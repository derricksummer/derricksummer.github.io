Go Again Support

No star ratings. Just remember whether you’d go again.

Go Again is a personal food diary that records each restaurant or café as “Go again”, “Maybe”, or “Once was enough”. Log a place in ten seconds, and when you’re wondering what to eat, let your past self make the call.

Features

  • Quick logging: search a place → rate it → save. Add photos, company, occasion, and notes only if you want.
  • Repeat visits: saving the same place again adds a visit instead of a duplicate.
  • Map: see every saved place as a photo pin.
  • What should I eat?: picks a “Go again” place you haven’t visited lately.
  • Reports and sharing: your regulars, neighbourhoods, and company by period, shared as a card or video.
  • Weekly nudge: Saturday morning, one favorite you haven’t visited in a while.

Private by design

  • No sign-up, no login.
  • Your records stay on your iPhone and your own iCloud. We have no server.
  • No ads, no tracking.
  • Place search sends your search text (and your location, if allowed) to Kakao or Apple Maps. See the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

  • I can’t find a place. Try searching by the place’s name. If it’s still missing, tap “Add it yourself” at the bottom of search and enter just the name, area, and category.
  • Will my records move to a new phone? Yes, if you use iCloud with the same Apple Account — install the App and it syncs automatically. Check that iCloud is on for Go Again in iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud.
  • Records don’t appear on my other device. Make sure both devices use the same Apple Account and are online. Sync can take a moment.
  • “Nearby” isn’t showing. It needs location access. Go to iPhone Settings > Go Again > Location and choose “While Using the App”.
  • I’m not getting reminders. Check the weekly nudge in the App’s settings and iPhone Settings > Notifications > Go Again. It only fires when a “Go again” place hasn’t been visited in a while.
  • How do I change a rating or delete a record? Open the place to change its rating, edit visits, or delete it.
  • How do I erase everything? Deleting the App removes data on the device. Remove iCloud data in iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.
