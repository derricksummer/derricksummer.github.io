RunCut Support

Run. Shoot. Keep.

RunCut is a running photo camera that puts today's distance, time and pace right on your photos and videos. Bring in runs from Apple Health or type one in.

Features

  • Straight to the camera: The app opens on the camera, like the built-in one, with today's run at the top.
  • Runs from Apple Health: Reads runs from your watch or running app and picks today's run automatically. Manual entry works too.
  • One-photo styles: STACK, SIDE, BIG, CENTER and TOP put your numbers on the photo in different ways.
  • Multi-photo run cuts: Take 2–4 photos and combine them as RUN CUT, RUN STATS, RUN STRIP or FILM ROLL.
  • Text and color: Change the font, color and size of the numbers and the photo's color look.
  • Video sharing: Turn the result into a short video with the distance counting up, ready for Stories.
  • Shapes: Save or share as 3:4, Story 9:16 or Feed 4:5.
  • Recent runs: Your saved run photos, grouped by month.
  • Three languages: English, 한국어, 日本語.

FAQ

  • My runs don't show up. Turn on workout and route access in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > RunCut, then tap the heart button above the camera.
  • I don't have a watch. Use “Add a run manually”: enter distance and time and start shooting.
  • Is there a shutter sound? Yes, the standard shutter sound plays.
  • No route on my photo. Indoor runs, runs recorded without GPS and manual runs have no route.
  • Is the video a real recording? No. It's a short clip made from your finished photo, with no audio track.
  • Photos won't save. Allow “Add Photos Only” in Settings > Apps > RunCut > Photos.
  • Does my Health data leave my phone? No. It's used only on your device and never for ads or analytics.
  • How do I change the language? Settings > Apps > RunCut > Language.
