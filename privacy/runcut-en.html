RunCut Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026

RunCut (the “app”) is a camera that puts your run on your photos and videos. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. No account, no server storage

The app has no sign-up or login and does not ask for your name, phone number or email. We do not run a server and do not store your photos, videos or runs on any server.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit) data

  • What we read: Only if you allow it, the app reads your running workouts (date, distance, duration) and workout routes. Pace is calculated on your device.
  • We never write: The app does not write or change any data in Apple Health.
  • Purpose: Only to show distance, time, pace and route on your photos and videos.
  • Never shared: Health data stays on your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else, not used for analytics, advertising or marketing, and never sold.
  • Manual runs: Runs you type in are stored only on your device and are not written to Apple Health.
  • You can change access anytime in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > RunCut.

3. Camera, photos and videos

  • Taking photos: The camera feed is processed only on your device. The app plays the standard shutter sound.
  • Saving: When you tap Save, the finished photo or video is added to Photos. The app asks for add-only access and never reads your library.
  • Importing: Only photos you pick in the system photo picker are passed to the app.
  • Run history: Saved results and run summaries are kept in the app's on-device storage, excluded from device backups, and never uploaded. They are deleted when you delete them in the app or delete the app.
  • Videos: Videos are short silent clips generated on your device from your finished photo. The microphone is not used.
  • Sharing: When you share, the photo or video goes to the app you choose, under that app's policy.

4. Information sent off the device

RecipientInformationPurpose
Google Firebase AnalyticsFeature usage (counts of photos taken, saves, shares; names of styles and color looks chosen), app language, a random per-install identifier, device model, OS version, approximate country/regionUsage statistics and improvement
Apple Maps (MapKit)The map area needed to draw a route mapShowing your route on a map
  • Firebase never receives your photos, videos, Health data (distance, time, routes) or text you type.

5. Ads and tracking

  • This version shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated first. Health data will never be used for advertising.

6. International transfer

The services in section 4 may process information outside your country (for example, in the United States) under Google's and Apple's policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 (or under 13 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Your choices

  • Delete runs and settings: delete them in Recent runs, or delete the app
  • Delete saved photos and videos: in the Photos app
  • Turn off Health access: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > RunCut
  • Stop usage statistics: delete the app

9. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the services in section 4 and the sharing you choose yourself, we do not provide your information to third parties.
